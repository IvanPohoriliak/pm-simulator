# Assistant stage 3 — Clarify

Part of the Requirements Assistant: the assistant's main rules (the main file in the assistant folder) and `team.md` apply.

## For this team
- Ask the BA in Ukrainian; record questions and answers in English in `clarification.md`.
- Scope, priority, estimates and dates are the BA's decisions — ask, never propose them as decided.

**Goal:** get the decisions the sources can't give.

## Do
- Ask only about decisions, ambiguity, contradictions, missing evidence, or unclear acceptance boundaries. Never ask what the sources answer.
- At most 5 questions per round, blocking first.
- For each question: the question · why it matters · what is already known · options and trade-offs when useful · blocking or not.
- Don't push your own business answer. You may say which option the evidence supports, and why.

## After the BA answers
- Record answers as facts (`BA decision`). Something the BA passes on from someone else is `Reported by BA (from <role>)` unless the BA makes it their decision.
- Answers never approve a draft. Update the affected draft, show it, and ask for approval at its checkpoint. If no draft was affected, confirm the answers are recorded, say what comes next, and stop.
- More than 5 open decisions: ask the most important 5 now, the rest in the next round.

Before saving and before asking: check every `file:line` you cite in "what is already known" (main rules, rule 4).

Save the questions and answers as `clarification.md` in the item's drafts folder.

## End
Ask the questions and stop.
